# RSC Advances: rules read live on 2026-11-08 (in-app browser)

Sources: https://www.rsc.org/publishing/journals/rsc-advances and
https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/rsc-advances

## Journal facts
- Gold open access; APC GBP 2,200, or no APC if the institution has an RSC open access agreement
  (check YTU / ULAKBIM EKUAL RSC quota with RSC Journal Finder before submission: TODO, user/library).
- Impact factor 6.1 (2025). First decision: 17 days (all), 27 days (peer reviewed). Indexed SCIE, Scopus, DOAJ, PubMed.
- Scope warning: "Papers that contain little or no chemistry ... are not within the scope of the journal."
  -> the paper must carry real chemistry: band assignments (lipid C-H, ester C=O, amide I/II, C-O), why
  sunflower vs peanut differ (fatty-acid profile, protein), chemical reading of model attributions.

## Article type: Paper
- No page limit; "appropriateness of length to content" is judged.
- Sections: Title, authors + affiliations, Abstract (single paragraph, ~50-250 words), Introduction,
  Experimental, Results and discussion, Conclusions, Author contributions (CRediT, strongly encouraged),
  Conflicts of interest (required; "There are no conflicts to declare"), Data availability statement
  (required), Acknowledgements (funding AND any use of AI tools declared here), Notes, References.
- Templates optional (Word and LaTeX article templates, Overleaf). With LaTeX, send native files + PDF.
- English, UK or US spelling consistently.

## References
- Vancouver numeric, superscript numbers after punctuation, numbered in order of first citation
  (tables/captions included). Multiple: 12,13 / 12,14,15 / 14-20 (en dash).
- Form: A. Name, B. Name and C. Name, Journal Title (CASSI abbreviation), year, volume, pages, DOI [optional].
  All authors' names and initials listed.
- Data citation form: A. Name, B. Name and C. Name, Repository, Dataset title, year, DOI.
- SI references may be listed at the end of the main list; note this in the data availability statement.

## Figures
- TIFF >= 600 dpi preferred (EPS/PDF accepted, converted to TIFF). Colour free.
- Width: single column 8.3 cm or double column 17.1 cm; height <= 23.3 cm; not larger than one page.
- Text legible; error bars where appropriate with uncertainty analysis; correct significant figures.
- Chemical structure captions/labels Arial/Helvetica 7 pt (use as the font guide for all figures).
- AI tools used to create figures must be declared (licensed training data, commercial reuse allowed);
  we draw every figure with our own matplotlib code, no generative AI.

## Table of contents entry (required at revision stage)
- Graphic max 8 cm wide x 4 cm high, TIFF >= 600 dpi, simple, no complex spectra, preferably not reused
  in the article; text 1-2 sentences, max 250 characters, not repeating title/abstract.

## Supplementary information
- Peer reviewed, submitted with the original manuscript, published as is. Spectral data ideally in
  JCAMP-DX; our data are already deposited (Zenodo) as xlsx, cite the deposit.
